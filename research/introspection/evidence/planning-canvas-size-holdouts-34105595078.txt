INTERPRETATION
These are controlled premium-flag size holdouts with the normal planning trace,
not random-seed controls. In both cases SELECT-CANVAS chooses half the
requested width and preserves the requested height; the resulting RIGHTMAX and
TOPMAX are one less than the selected canvas dimensions. The 1920x1080 run also
shows SET-UP-SCREEN-SIZE retaining *PIC-HIGH*=768 before SELECT-CANVAS changes
it to 1080. This supports a measured high-resolution path, but does not prove a
universal half-width rule for every display, aspect ratio, small-image setting,
or gallery build.
