before RPARSE, creates the private fill map, then enters real figure drawing.
The initialized path reaches PREP-LINE and STORE-IN-FILE, unlike the isolated
SCREEN-AND-STORE call that stopped at unbound MPLAN. STORE-IN-FILE is now the
high-fanout edge; the next probe leaves it unwrapped to expose its downstream
line/brush/plot calls. This is runtime evidence, not a claim that every
STORE-IN-FILE output byte has already been matched.
